Both videos are **silent**. Add sound when you post. The captions are part of the video, so it works muted too.

## Adding sound

- **TikTok:** after you upload, tap **Sounds** and pick a trending track. Trending sounds help the video get shown. Upbeat lo-fi, sea shanties or anything with a strong drop works well with the monster fight at the end.
- **YouTube Shorts:** tap **Add sound** in the Shorts editor.
- **YouTube (regular video):** in YouTube Studio, open the video, go to **Editor → Audio**, and pick a free track from the Audio Library. Or add music in CapCut before uploading.
- **CapCut (free, phone or computer):** import the video, add a track from its music library, and line the beat drop up with "…and land a LEGEND."

## Tips

- Pin a comment with the itch.io link on YouTube, and put it in your TikTok bio, since TikTok captions can't hold clickable links.
- Post the vertical version to TikTok, Shorts and Reels on the same day.
- The first 2 seconds decide whether people keep watching, so the vertical trailer opens on the monster fight.
