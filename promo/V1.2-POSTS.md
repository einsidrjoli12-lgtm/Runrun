## Upload checklist

1. itch.io → your game → **Edit game** → Uploads: upload the new `bobber-and-brine-itch.zip` and delete the old zip, or use **Replace**. Keep "This file will be played in the browser" ticked.
2. Save, open the game page and play a few casts to check it loads. Old saves carry over.
3. **Devlog** (game page → Devlog → Create devlog): paste the post below, put the wide image at the top, type **Update**, publish.
4. Post the socials. Post the code cards over the next few days, not all at once.

## itch.io devlog

**Title:** Bobber & Brine v1.2: Pearls, skins, trophies and a fairer crew 🎣

```
hey everyone! v1.2 is out, and it's the biggest update so far 🎣

🫧 PEARLS
there's a new currency called pearls. you earn them by fishing yourself:
- perfect catches (6% chance, doubled during storms)
- catching epic and legendary fish
- your first catch of every species
- finishing goals and completing a water's logbook
- landing legendary monsters (+100 the first time!)
- a daily bonus for your first perfect catch each day

pearls never reset, not even when you retire. if you played before, you already got pearls for everything you've done so far 🎁

🎩 SKINS
the new Skins tab has 31 cosmetics: bobbers, rod wraps, hats, dock companions, lanterns and crew boats, from Common to Legendary. some favorites: the rubber duck bobber, the lava rod, the crown, and an otter that sits on your dock. skins are just for looks, they don't change your catches or money.

🏆 TROPHIES
land Old Mossback, Grandfather Marlin or The Leviathan and their trophy goes up on your dock. you can't buy these, you have to earn them.

🎁 EXCLUSIVE SKINS
follow Bobber & Brine on TikTok and Bobber & Brine Game on Instagram and YouTube. each one has a secret code for a free exclusive skin. type it in Settings → Code.

📖 THE LOGBOOK HAS PICTURES NOW
every one of the 36 fish (and the 6 legends) has its own drawing. fish you haven't caught yet show up as a dark silhouette, so you can see what you're still missing 👀

⚖️ FAIRER CREW
a lot of you said the same thing: later on, deckhands made so much money that fishing yourself stopped mattering. so:
- deckhands, training and crew gear now cost more in each new water
- to make up for it, new waters are cheaper to unlock
- fame stars now start at $1M instead of $1B (careers already in progress were converted, so you keep the same stars you were working toward)
your crew is still worth hiring and still earns while you're away. it just doesn't take over anymore.

🛠️ FIXES AND SMALL STUFF
- cleaner layout on phones: one row of stats, smaller labels, slimmer ability buttons
- pop-ups on phones are a slim bar now and don't cover the upgrade you're about to tap
- crew boats show one money number at a time instead of a pile of them
- fixed a bug where wiping your save gave you money from your old save on the first few goals

your save carries over like always. thanks so much for playing and for all the feedback, it really shaped this update ❤️

what should I add next? tell me in the comments 🐟

▶ play: https://einarsgames.itch.io/bobberbrine
```
