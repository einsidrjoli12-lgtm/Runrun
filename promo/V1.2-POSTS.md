## itch.io devlog

**Title:** Bobber & Brine v1.2: Pearls, skins and trophies 🎣

**Post type:** Announcement · put `promo/update-v1.2-wide-1920x1080.png` at the top.

```
hey everyone! v1.2 is here and it's all about making your angler look cool 😎

🫧 PEARLS
there's a new currency called pearls. you earn them by fishing yourself:
- perfect catches (6% chance, doubled during storms)
- catching epic and legendary fish
- your first catch of every species
- finishing goals and completing a water's logbook
- landing legendary monsters (+100 the first time!)
- a daily bonus for your first perfect catch each day

pearls never reset, not even when you retire. if you played before, you already got pearls for everything you've done so far 🎁

🎩 SKINS
the new Skins tab has 31 cosmetics: bobbers, rod wraps, hats, dock companions, lanterns and crew boats, from Common to Legendary. some favorites: the rubber duck bobber, the lava rod, the crown, and an otter that sits on your dock. everything shows up right in the game.

🏆 TROPHIES
land Old Mossback, Grandfather Marlin or The Leviathan and their trophy goes up on your dock. you can't buy these, you have to earn them.

🎁 EXCLUSIVE SKINS
follow Bobber & Brine on TikTok and Bobber & Brine Game on Instagram and YouTube. each one has a secret code for a free exclusive skin. type it in Settings → Code.

skins are just for looks, they don't change your catches or money. your save carries over like always.

what skin do you want next? tell me in the comments 🐟

▶ play: https://einarsgames.itch.io/bobberbrine
```
