## Pearls 🫧

A second currency, only for cosmetics. Pearls **never reset**: not when you unlock a new water, and not when you retire. You earn them by **fishing yourself**. Your crew and offline time don't earn pearls, so pearls reward playing.

| How you earn pearls | Chance | Pearls |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect catch | 6% (12% during storms) | +1 |
| Catch an Epic fish | 25% | +3 |
| Catch a Legendary fish | always | +8 |
| First catch of a new species | always | +5 (36 species = 180 total) |
| Complete a goal | always | +10 (52 goals = 520 total) |
| Complete a water's logbook | always | +50 (6 waters = 300 total) |
| Land a legendary monster | always | +40, plus +100 the first time you land each one |
| Daily bonus: your first perfect catch of the day | once a day | +10 |

### How fast that is (estimate)

- **First hour:** about 100 pearls, because new species and early goals add up fast. Your first Common skin comes after roughly 30–40 minutes.
- **After that:** about 35–45 pearls per hour of active fishing, plus goals, legends and daily bonuses.
- **Rough time per tier:** Common ~1.5 h · Rare ~4–5 h · Epic ~10–12 h · Legendary ~25–30 h of active play.

These numbers are a starting point and are easy to tune after players try it.

## Rules

- Everything is **cosmetic only**: no skin changes catches, money or odds.
- Skins are **kept forever**, through retiring and new waters.
- One item per slot is equipped at a time (bobber, rod wrap, hat, companion, lantern, boats, trophy), changed in a new **Wardrobe**.
- Codes can be shared freely, since they only unlock cosmetics.
