How to play

  1. Tap the water (or press Space) to cast your line.
  2. Wait for the bobber to dip and the ! to appear, then tap for a perfect catch. Tap too early and the fish swims off.
  3. Every fish earns money. Spend it in the shop on tackle, a crew that fishes for you, specials and new waters.
  4. Sometimes a legendary monster bites. Tap as fast as you can to fill the meter before it snaps your line.
  5. Finish goals for permanent income bonuses, and retire once you've earned $1M to start a stronger career.
  6. Perfect catches, new fish, goals and legends earn pearls. Spend them on looks in the Skins tab.
  7. Keys 1–4 fire your abilities. Your progress saves automatically.

What's new in v1.2

  • Pearls 🫧, a new currency for cosmetics. Earn them from perfect catches, new species, rare fish, goals, legends and a daily bonus. Pearls never reset.
  • A new Skins tab: bobbers, rod wraps, hats, dock companions, lanterns and crew boats, from Common to Legendary.
  • Trophies for your dock when you land Old Mossback, Grandfather Marlin and The Leviathan.
  • Platform codes: follow Bobber & Brine on TikTok and Bobber & Brine Game on Instagram and YouTube for codes that unlock exclusive skins. Enter them in Settings → Code.
  • Rebalance: deckhands now cost more in each new water, so your own fishing stays worth it. To make up for it, new waters are cheaper to unlock and the first fame star comes at $1M.
